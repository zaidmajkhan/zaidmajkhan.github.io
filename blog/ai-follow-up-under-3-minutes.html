Getting AI follow-up under 3 minutes

"We need AI for lead follow-up" usually means "our leads go cold before anyone responds." The fix is rarely a smarter model — it's a faster, more reliable process with AI handling the parts humans shouldn't be doing manually.

Step 1: Map the trigger

Every automation starts with a single, unambiguous trigger. For most small businesses that's one of:

  • New row in a form spreadsheet
  • Webhook from website form or CRM
  • Missed call event from phone system

If the trigger is fuzzy ("check the inbox every hour"), the automation will be fuzzy too.

Step 2: Personalize with context, not creativity

The first message doesn't need to be clever. It needs to reference what the lead actually asked about — the service, the vehicle, the appointment type. Pull fields from the form submission and inject them into a template. AI helps when the input is unstructured (a free-text message), but templated fields cover 80% of cases.

Step 3: Route and notify in parallel

While the SMS goes out, three things should happen simultaneously:

  • Lead logged to CRM with timestamp and source
  • Assigned rep notified (SMS, Slack, or email)
  • Follow-up sequence scheduled if no reply within X hours

Step 4: Handle edge cases upfront

Duplicate submissions, wrong numbers, after-hours vs business-hours messaging, opt-out requests — these break naive automations. Map them in the design phase, not after launch.

The result

When this pattern is implemented cleanly, "speed-to-lead" stops being a KPI you report on monthly. It becomes a property of the system — always on, always fast, always logged.
