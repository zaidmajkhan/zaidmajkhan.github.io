About

I grew up watching my family navigate a healthcare system that felt designed to lose people in the cracks. Complex processes, poor information flow, broken hand-offs. An engineer looks at that and sees fixable problems.

I'm studying Industrial and Systems Engineering at Texas A&M because ISE is specifically about making complex systems work — reducing waste, improving flow, designing processes that scale. That's what healthcare needs.

In the meantime, I apply those same principles to small businesses through AI automation. Every client is practice for thinking in systems. Every process I redesign teaches me something about how things actually break down.

Mission: build a healthcare systems company that actually moves the needle.
